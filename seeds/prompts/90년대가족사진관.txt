Using the attached pet photo, create a photograph of this exact pet as a
1990s Korean family photo studio portrait. Vertical 4:5 canvas.

IDENTITY LOCK (most important)
The pet must be the SAME individual as the attached photo — identical
coat color, identical markings and their exact placement, identical ear
type, shape and length, same muzzle length, same eye color, same face
proportions. Never change the breed silhouette.
Fully photorealistic.

THE POSE — stiff and formal, exactly like the era
- Head and chest portrait, the pet sitting upright dead center in the
  frame, facing the camera straight on
- Slightly awkward formal posture, as if being held still for the shot
- Head tilted very slightly to one side
- Mouth closed, neutral polite expression, ears in their natural relaxed
  position, looking directly into the lens
- Framed a little too tightly, with not quite enough headroom above

THE OUTFIT
A small knitted sweater vest or a stiff collared shirt with a tiny
clip-on bow tie, in dated 90s colors — mustard yellow, dusty rose, or
muted teal, with a small geometric or argyle pattern. The garment looks
slightly too formal and a little uncomfortable.

THE BACKDROP
A painted canvas studio backdrop in the style specified above, hung
behind the subject:
- Visibly a physical painted cloth, with soft airbrushed blending and
  faint fabric wrinkles near the edges
- Slightly cheesy and dated, with an obvious vignette darkening toward
  the corners
- The bottom edge of the backdrop is faintly visible where it meets the
  floor

THE LIGHTING — this is what sells the era
- Hard, flat, direct on-camera flash from the front, flattening the face
- A blown-out bright hotspot on the forehead and the nose
- A hard-edged shadow cast on the backdrop directly behind the subject
- Slight red-eye glow in the pupils
- Overall overexposed on the highlights, with the shadows crushed and
  muddy

FILM AND PRINT CHARACTER
- Shot on expired 90s color film: warm reddish-orange color cast,
  slightly faded blacks, low contrast in the midtones
- Visible film grain throughout
- Soft, slightly out-of-focus quality overall — not tack sharp anywhere
- Mild chromatic fringing at the edges of the frame
- The whole image has the feel of a physical print that has been sitting
  in an album for thirty years: subtle surface texture, faint scuffs, a
  very slight yellowing

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, logos, dates or watermarks anywhere.

STYLE
An authentic 1990s Korean photo studio portrait. Stiff, formal, slightly
awkward and unintentionally charming. It must look like a real scanned
photograph from a family album — never modern, never polished, never
digitally clean.
