Using the attached pet photo, create a photograph of this exact pet
peeking out from inside a cosmos flower field in autumn, shot from a low
angle against a clear blue sky. Vertical 4:5 canvas.

IDENTITY LOCK (most important)
The pet must be the SAME individual as the attached photo — identical
coat color, identical markings and their exact placement, identical ear
type, shape and length, same muzzle length, same eye color, same face
proportions. Never change the breed silhouette. Fully photorealistic.

THE FLOWERS
- Tall cosmos flowers on thin green stems, in the cosmos color written
  above (주황 = warm orange sulfur cosmos; 분홍 = soft pink; 흰색 = white;
  알록달록 = a natural mix of pink, white and magenta)
- The flowers surround the pet on all sides and fill the lower two thirds
  of the frame — some in front of the pet, softly out of focus and
  partially overlapping the chest and chin, some beside the face at eye
  level, some behind
- Feathery cosmos leaves and buds mixed in, a few petals catching the
  light and glowing translucent
- The pet is IN the field, not placed on top of it: stems cross in front
  of the body, and the fur touches the petals

THE POSE
- Head and upper chest visible, the rest hidden below the flowers
- Head slightly lifted, looking a little past the camera or straight
  into the lens with a calm, curious expression
- Mouth gently closed or a soft relaxed open mouth, ears in their natural
  position, nothing exaggerated

THE SKY AND LIGHT
- Clear deep blue autumn sky filling the top of the frame, no clouds or
  just a faint wisp
- Warm late-afternoon sunlight from behind and to one side, rim-lighting
  the fur and the petal edges with a golden glow
- Gentle lens flare or a soft haze near the sun is welcome, but the face
  stays clearly lit and readable

CAMERA
Shot from below, camera almost at flower height looking slightly up at
the pet. A 35mm lens at a wide aperture: sharp focus on the eyes and
nose, the front flowers melting into soft warm blur, the sky smooth and
clean. Natural color with slightly lifted shadows and a warm film-like
cast, fine grain, a touch of imperfect handheld framing. No heavy
filters.

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

STYLE
A real autumn snapshot taken in a cosmos field, the kind of photo used
as a profile picture. Warm, bright and nostalgic. It must look like an
actual photograph, never a digital illustration or a painted composite.
