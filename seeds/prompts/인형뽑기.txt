Using the attached pet photo, create a real photograph looking through
the glass of a Japanese-style claw machine, where THIS EXACT PET — the
real, living animal, not a toy — sits inside among the plush prizes as if
it were the prize everyone is trying to win. Vertical 4:5 canvas.

THE PET — a real animal, photographed
The pet is the real dog from the photo, alive and photorealistic:
- Same face, same expression, same eyes and nose, same coat colors and
  markings in the same positions, same ear shape, same coat length and
  texture. Do not stylize, do not turn it into a plush, figurine, cartoon
  or 3D render.
- Real fur with individual strands catching the light, real wet nose,
  real glossy eyes with natural catchlights
- Sitting or lying comfortably front and center against the glass,
  looking at the camera, in sharp focus — this is the hero
- Natural size: it fills a good part of the machine, clearly bigger than
  the plush toys around it

THE MACHINE INTERIOR
- Around and behind the pet, a pile of soft generic plush toys in muted
  pastel colors (simple round bears, bunnies, blobs) — plain and
  understated so the real pet stands out. No plush that looks like the
  pet, no characters, no branded toys.
- The plushes are arranged naturally at varied angles, some half-buried,
  never a neat grid. The ones further back are softer and out of focus,
  slightly dimmer, receding into the depth of the machine
- A metal three-pronged claw hangs from above, partly in frame at the top,
  slightly open, its cable running up out of view
- The interior is lit by cool bright LED strips along the top edges
- The back wall of the machine is a flat colored panel with no readable
  text
- The prize chute opening is visible at one lower corner, dark inside

THE GLASS — do not skip this, it makes the image work
We are looking through the machine's glass panel:
- A soft diagonal reflection streaks across the glass, catching the
  ambient light of the arcade
- A few faint fingerprints and smudges near the middle of the pane
- The glass adds a very slight haze and softening to everything behind it
- A dim ghosted reflection of arcade lights from behind the viewer
- A thin metal frame edge visible at one side of the frame

THE MACHINE EXTERIOR
The frame and trim of the machine in the color specified above, glossy
painted metal with a few small scuffs. Blurred neon arcade lighting in
the reflection — pinks, blues and warm yellows.

LIGHT AND CAMERA
Shot close, straight through the glass at the pet's eye level, with a
50mm lens on a full-frame camera. The pet in sharp focus, the toys behind
it falling soft, the reflections adding depth. Cool interior LED light
mixed with warm arcade glow. Fine natural grain, true-to-life colors,
natural imperfect framing, no filters.

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, logos, prices or watermarks anywhere in the
image.

STYLE
A real candid photograph taken at an arcade — the kind of photo a friend
would snap on a mirrorless camera and post without editing. Bright,
playful and a little absurd: a real dog calmly sitting inside a claw
machine. It must look like an actual photograph, never an illustration,
render or plush-toy version of the pet.
