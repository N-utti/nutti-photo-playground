Using the attached pet photo, create an extreme wide-angle close-up
photograph of this exact pet sniffing directly into the camera lens.
Vertical 4:5 canvas.

THE LENS EFFECT — this is the entire point
The camera has a very wide-angle lens and is placed only a few
centimetres from the pet's nose. The resulting perspective distortion
must be strong and physically correct:
- The NOSE is the closest thing to the lens and is therefore hugely
  exaggerated — it dominates the center of the frame, taking up roughly a
  third of the image width
- Because the eyes are further from the lens, they appear noticeably
  SMALLER and are pushed back and outward, sitting high and wide apart on
  either side of the enormous muzzle
- The forehead and the top of the head recede sharply and appear narrow
  and compressed
- The body and front legs are far away and tiny at the bottom of the
  frame, dropping off steeply
- Everything falls away rapidly from the nose in all directions

CRITICAL: the nose is not simply drawn larger. The whole head is
distorted by proximity — near things huge, far things small. Keep this
consistent across the entire face.

IDENTITY LOCK
The pet must be the SAME individual as the attached photo — identical
coat color, identical markings and their exact placement, identical ear
type, identical eye color, same muzzle shape. Never change the breed
silhouette. Fully photorealistic.

THE NOSE — the hero of the image, render it in full detail
- Wet, glossy black or brown nose leather with a visible textured surface
  of fine bumps and ridges, like tiny cobblestones
- Two large nostrils, deep and dark, slightly asymmetrical
- A clear vertical groove down the center, running into the philtrum
- Small soft specular highlights across the wet surface
- Fine whiskers sprouting from the muzzle, catching the light, some
  crossing the frame edges

THE FACE
- The pet looks directly into the lens, eyes soft and curious
- Slightly cross-eyed, focusing on the very close camera
- Calm, gentle, inquisitive expression — mouth closed
- Fine fur detail visible on the muzzle, individual hairs around the nose
- Head positioned slightly off-center for a natural candid feel

BACKGROUND AND LIGHT
- A plain, clean, softly out-of-focus indoor wall in light neutral grey
  or off-white, with nothing else in frame
- Soft diffused natural window light from the front, gentle and even, no
  harsh shadows
- Natural muted color, slightly desaturated, no filters

CAMERA
Shot on a full-frame camera with a very wide lens at close focus. The
nose is in sharp focus; the eyes and the top of the head fall slightly
soft; the background is fully blurred. Visible fine grain. Slightly
imperfect handheld framing, as if grabbed in the moment.

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

STYLE
A real candid snapshot. Funny, intimate and slightly absurd — the kind of
photo taken when a curious dog walks right up to the phone.
