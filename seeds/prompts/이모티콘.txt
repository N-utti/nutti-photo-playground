LIKENESS (most important)
Every sticker must show the SAME individual pet from the photo — identical
coat color, markings, ear shape, muzzle length and face proportions.
Keep it recognizable as this specific pet, not a generic breed illustration.

STYLE
- Soft, semi-realistic 3D-rendered plush look — fluffy fur texture,

  shadow, like a real printed sticker

LAYOUT

- Each cell: the pet's expression on top, its caption text below
