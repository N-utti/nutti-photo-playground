Create a photograph of a glass snow globe containing a miniature figurine
of the pet in the attached photo. Vertical 4:5 canvas.

THE PET FIGURINE INSIDE
- A small hand-painted ceramic figurine of the pet, sitting upright in
  the center of the globe's base, facing the viewer
- Keep it recognizable: same coat colors, same markings in the same
  positions, same ear type and shape, same muzzle length, same sitting
  posture as the attached photo
- Slightly simplified and softened as figurines are, with a subtle
  hand-painted quality — visible brush texture, a soft glaze sheen,
  tiny imperfections
- Calm, gentle expression, eyes painted with small highlights

THE GLASS — this is what makes it a snow globe, do not skip it
The pet is seen THROUGH a thick sphere of glass filled with liquid.
Everything inside must be optically affected:
- Strong REFRACTION: the scene inside is magnified slightly in the center
  and curves and compresses toward the edges of the sphere. Straight
  lines inside bend.
- A bright curved specular highlight arcs across the upper left of the
  glass, where the light source reflects
- A softer secondary reflection near the lower right
- A thin bright ring of light traces the outer rim of the sphere
- The very edge of the glass is darker and denser, with the interior
  distorted into a thin compressed band
- A faint cool blue-green tint through the water, slightly desaturating
  the figurine compared to the world outside the globe
- Tiny air bubbles clinging near the top inside surface
- Faint dust and fingerprints on the outer glass

THE SNOW
- Hundreds of small white flakes suspended mid-fall throughout the water,
  scattered at different depths
- Flakes near the front of the sphere are larger and out of focus; those
  further back are tiny and sharper
- A few flakes have settled on the figurine's back and head, and on the
  base
- The flakes drift and swirl slightly, caught in motion

THE MINIATURE SCENE
Build the theme written above as a tiny diorama around the figurine,
scaled to fit inside the globe — small sculpted trees, buildings, rocks
or flowers, all in the same hand-painted ceramic style. Snow or ground
cover across the base. Keep it uncluttered so the pet remains the focus.

THE BASE
A turned wooden base in warm walnut, with a simple decorative moulding
and a small blank brass plaque on the front, with no text on it.

THE SETTING
The snow globe sits on a wooden table beside a window. Softly blurred in
the background: a warm room, out-of-focus fairy lights, a mug, the
suggestion of a windowsill.

LIGHT AND CAMERA
Warm directional window light from one side, plus soft fairy-light bokeh
behind, creating rich reflections across the glass. Shot close at the
globe's eye level with an 85mm lens. The figurine's face in sharp focus,
the front surface of the glass and the background falling soft. Natural
warm color, fine grain, no filters.

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

STYLE
A real photograph of a real snow globe on a table. Warm, nostalgic and
tactile. It must look like actual glass and water — never a digital
illustration, never a flat composite of a dog with snow added.
