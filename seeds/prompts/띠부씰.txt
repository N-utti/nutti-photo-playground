die-cut sticker card featuring this exact pet, in the style of Korean

IDENTITY LOCK
The pet must be the SAME individual as the attached photo — identical
coat color, identical markings and their exact placement, identical ear
type, shape and length, same muzzle length, same eye color, same face
proportions. Never change the breed silhouette. Fully photorealistic.

THE STICKER — this is a cut-out, not a rectangular photo
- The pet is photographed against a pure white background, then cut out

THE POSE
The pet sits or stands facing the camera head-on, body compact and
centered, looking directly into the lens with a bright cheerful

position. Simple and clear — the silhouette should read well as a cut-out

THE BACKING CARD
- The sticker is mounted on a plain white rectangular backing card,
  filling most of the frame with an even margin on all sides
- The card has a very subtle paper texture and slightly rounded corners

THE LABEL — top left of the backing card, outside the sticker

TEXT RENDERING — CRITICAL
Render the number and the name EXACTLY as written in the fields above,

STYLE
A real photograph of a real collectible sticker card. Clean, bright and
