Using the attached pet photo, create an underwater photograph of this
exact pet swimming beneath the surface of a bright turquoise pool,
shot from below looking upward. Vertical 4:5 canvas.

THE CAMERA POSITION
The camera is deep in the water, angled up toward the surface. The pet is
above us, swimming through the water between the camera and the light.
- We see the pet mostly from below and slightly to one side
- The rippling water surface is visible above, glowing bright with the
  sun refracting through it into scattered moving highlights
- The camera is close enough that the pet fills a good portion of the
  frame

IDENTITY LOCK
The pet must be the SAME individual as the attached photo — identical
coat color, identical markings and their exact placement, identical ear
type, shape and length, same muzzle length, same eye color.
Never change the breed silhouette. Fully photorealistic.

THE POSE — natural and asymmetrical
A live animal caught mid-stroke, never posed or mirrored.
- The body angles diagonally through the frame, rotated a little off any
  clean axis
- All four legs are at DIFFERENT positions in the paddle cycle — one
  front paw reaching forward, the other tucked back, the hind legs
  offset from each other. Never symmetrical.
- The head is turned slightly, chin lifted toward the surface
- The tail streams behind and off to one side
- The body has a gentle curve to it, mid-motion
- Positioned OFF-CENTER in the frame, with uneven water on either side

WATER PHYSICS — this is what makes it believable
- The fur is WET and flattened against the body, darker and heavier than
  when dry, clumping into pointed strands. It must NOT look fluffy, dry
  or backlit-fuzzy.
- Long soft fur, if the pet has it, drifts slowly and floats outward,
  suspended by the water
- A dense trail of small silver AIR BUBBLES streams from the fur, the
  muzzle and behind the paws, rising toward the surface in an irregular
  scattered pattern
- A few larger bubbles escape from the nose
- Where the paws push, faint swirls and distortion in the water

LIGHT — sunlight through water
- Bright sun above the surface, sending soft shifting caustic light
  patterns rippling across the pet's back and body
- The underside is in cooler shadow, lit only by blue light scattering up
  from the pool floor
- A bright bloom of light around the surface directly above the pet
- Light rays fanning down through the water in soft visible shafts
- Deep saturated turquoise water, growing darker and hazier toward the
  frame edges

UNDERWATER OPTICS — do not skip these
- A distinct blue-cyan color cast over EVERYTHING, including the pet.
  Warm tones are heavily muted; reds and oranges are nearly gone.
- Reduced contrast and visible haze, especially toward the edges of the
  frame — water scatters light and softens distance
- Small suspended particles drifting in the water, catching the light
- Slight refractive distortion at the frame edges
- The pet is NOT sharper, brighter or more saturated than the water
  around it. It is subject to the same haze and color cast.

CAMERA
Shot on an underwater housing with a wide lens. Fast shutter freezing the
motion, with very slight blur at the paw tips. Sharp focus on the face,
falling soft toward the tail. Visible grain, natural imperfect framing,
as if grabbed in the moment. No filters.

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

STYLE
A real underwater photograph. Bright, weightless and joyful. It must look
like a genuine shot taken in a pool — never a composite, never a digital
illustration.
