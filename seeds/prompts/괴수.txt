Using the attached pet photo, create an epic kaiju monster movie poster
featuring this exact pet as the colossal creature. Vertical 4:5 canvas.

THE JOKE — read this first
The entire image is played completely straight, as a serious, ominous,
apocalyptic kaiju film. The city is terrified. The atmosphere is heavy
and dramatic. Every single element treats this as a world-ending event.
The ONLY thing that is not serious is the creature itself — which is an
ordinary, soft, happy little dog, four hundred feet tall, having the best
day of its life. The comedy comes entirely from that contrast. Never wink
at the audience: the production values stay dead serious.

IDENTITY LOCK
The creature must be the SAME individual as the attached photo —
identical coat color, identical markings and their exact placement,
identical ear type, shape and length, same muzzle length, same eye color,
same face proportions. Never change the breed silhouette.
It is a completely ordinary dog at monstrous scale. NO scales, NO horns,
NO spines, NO claws, NO fangs, NO glowing eyes, NO armor plating, NO
muscular monster anatomy. Just the dog. Enormous.

THE CREATURE'S DEMEANOR — the punchline
- Standing upright on all fours in a dramatic hero pose, but the pose is
  slightly goofy: head tilted with curiosity, one ear flopped
- Mouth open in a happy pant, tongue lolling out to one side
- Bright friendly eyes, completely oblivious to the chaos
- Soft fluffy fur ruffled by the wind — visibly soft and huggable against
  the hard, grim city
- It looks like it is about to sit for a treat. It is not attacking.
  It is delighted to be here.

THE ATMOSPHERE — this is where the epic tone lives
- Deep night, heavy fog and thick smoke rolling through the streets
- The creature emerges from the mist, its lower body swallowed by haze
- Strong backlighting from behind the creature, throwing the front of the
  body into partial silhouette with a cold blue-white glow around it
- Dense volumetric light beams cutting through the fog
- Cold teal-and-blue color grading with deep crushed blacks, punctuated
  by warm orange emergency lights far below
- Distant neon signage glowing pink and cyan through the murk
- Ash and debris drifting slowly through the air

THE CITY
- A vast dense metropolis at night seen from a high dramatic angle,
  skyscrapers stretching to the horizon
- The creature towers over every building, head above the skyline
- Tiny lit office windows across the towers for scale
- Streams of red tail lights fleeing along the avenues far below
- Several small helicopters circling, searchlights sweeping the creature
- Distant plumes of smoke, sirens implied by scattered flashing lights

THE SCENE
Stage the moment written above with full cinematic gravity.
If breathing fire: a massive roaring plume erupting into the sky,
illuminating the fog from within — but the dog's expression stays
cheerful, as if it just sneezed.

CAMERA
Wide anamorphic cinematic lens, low dramatic angle looking up. Shallow
atmospheric depth. Heavy film grain, subtle lens flare, high dynamic
range. Slight chromatic aberration at the edges.

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

STYLE
A real blockbuster monster movie poster. Photorealistic, grim, epic and
atmospheric — with an extremely cute dog at the center of it.
The dog must be rendered as a real photographic dog, never stylized,
never cartoonish, never anime. Real fur, real dog anatomy, real eyes.
