Using the attached pet photo, create a photograph of this exact pet
pressing its face and both front paws flat against a pane of glass,
shot from the other side of the glass. Vertical 4:5 canvas.

IDENTITY LOCK
The pet must be the SAME individual as the attached photo — identical
coat color, identical markings and their exact placement, identical ear
type, shape and length, same muzzle length, same eye color.
Never change the breed silhouette. Fully photorealistic.

THE CONTACT — the entire point, get this right
The pet is pushing hard against the glass. The camera is directly on the
other side, very close. Everything touching the glass must be visibly
COMPRESSED:
- The NOSE is squashed flat against the pane, the nose leather spread
  wide and pushed slightly upward, its wet surface smeared into contact
- The CHEEKS and the fur around the muzzle splay outward where they meet
  the glass, flattened and fanned
- The LIPS are pressed and slightly distorted, with the tongue partly
  squished against the pane
- BOTH FRONT PAWS are planted flat on the glass on either side of the
  face, toes spread wide, the paw pads mashed flat and pale where the
  weight presses on them. Every pad and the fur between them is clearly
  visible.
- Wherever fur or skin touches the glass it turns slightly paler and
  loses texture from the pressure

THE GLASS
- Smudges, nose prints and paw smears left on the pane around the contact
  points, with faint moisture and condensation halos
- A soft diagonal reflection streaking across the glass surface
- Very slight haze and softening from the pane itself
- Small dust specks and old fingerprints

THE POSE AND EXPRESSION
- Standing on hind legs, chest against the glass, head-on to the camera
- Eyes wide, bright and eager, looking straight through the glass into
  the lens
- Mouth open, tongue out and squashed slightly sideways
- Ears in their natural relaxed position, one slightly higher than the
  other
- Slightly off-center in the frame, head tilted a little — a candid
  moment, never a symmetrical pose

THE BACKGROUND
Behind the pet, a softly blurred ordinary indoor room — a sofa, a
doorway, warm daylight from a window. Completely out of focus so the
pressed face dominates.

LIGHT AND CAMERA
Soft natural daylight from the pet's side, gently rim-lighting the fur
along the edges. Shot very close with a 35mm lens, the glass filling the
frame. Sharp focus on the nose and paw pads, falling soft toward the
ears. Natural color, fine grain, slightly imperfect handheld framing,
no filters.

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

STYLE
A real candid snapshot taken through a window or a door. Funny, warm and
immediate — the look of a dog desperate to come inside.
