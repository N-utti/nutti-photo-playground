Using the attached pet photo, create a warm, realistic photograph of this
exact pet working as the profession written above. Vertical 4:5 canvas.

IDENTITY LOCK (most important)
The pet must be the SAME individual as the attached photo — identical
coat color, identical markings and their exact placement, identical ear
type, shape and length, same muzzle length, same eye color, same face
proportions. Never change the breed silhouette.
Fully photorealistic, like a real photograph of a real dog.

THE SETUP
- The pet stands or sits upright behind a counter, desk, or workstation
  appropriate to the job, with its front paws resting naturally on the
  surface — as if it has been doing this job for years
- Head and upper body fill the frame, shot at the pet's eye level
- The face stays a completely normal, unmodified dog face: real canine
  anatomy, normal eye size, natural expression. Never anthropomorphized,
  never given human eyes or a human mouth.
- Expression: focused and professional, but warm — mouth slightly open,
  ears in their natural relaxed position, looking directly at the camera
  as if a customer just walked in

THE UNIFORM
Dress the pet in real, well-fitted work clothing for that profession —
the actual garments a person in that job would wear, tailored to a dog's
body. Include the small authentic details that sell it: the apron and its
ties, the collar and buttons, the badge or name tag, the rolled sleeves,
the cap or helmet sitting slightly askew. The fabric should look worn-in
and real, with natural creases and a little wear, not costume-shop new.

THE WORKPLACE
Build the actual environment of that job around the pet, filled with the
real tools and clutter of the trade:
- The equipment that profession uses, arranged as if genuinely in use
  mid-shift — something in progress, not staged
- Authentic background detail: shelves, hanging tools, notes pinned to a
  board, worn surfaces, the small mess of a real working space
- One or two tiny charming touches that hint a dog works here — a paw
  print on a surface, a chew toy tucked beside the tools, a bone-shaped
  keychain
- The background falls softly out of focus, but stays clearly readable

LIGHT AND MOOD
Warm natural light — window light, or the practical lamps of that
workspace. Soft shadows, gentle highlights on the fur. Cozy, lived-in,
slightly nostalgic. It should feel like a documentary portrait of a
craftsperson at work.

CAMERA
Shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm lens at a wide aperture. Sharp
focus on the eyes, creamy background bokeh. Natural color, subtle film
grain, no filters.

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

STYLE
Photorealistic environmental portrait photography. Warm, authentic and
charming. It must look like a real photograph, not a digital
illustration and not a cartoon.
