방식: 에칭 아트
  ① 프리푸어 — 붓는 것만으로 만든 심플한 실루엣
  ② 에칭 아트 — 꼬치로 그린 세밀한 얼굴 (제일 잘 나와요!)

Create a photograph of a latte in a ceramic cup, where the surface art
depicts the pet in the attached photo. Vertical 4:5 canvas.

THIS IS MADE OF MILK FOAM — most important
The image on the coffee is NOT drawn, printed, or stamped on. It is
formed entirely from steamed milk foam sitting on espresso crema.
- Only TWO tones exist: the warm caramel-brown of the crema, and the soft
  off-white of the microfoam. Nothing else.
- No black, no color, no fine shading, no gradients beyond what foam
  naturally makes
- Every edge where foam meets crema is soft and slightly feathered, never
  a crisp printed line
- The foam has real physical volume — subtle thickness, a faint sheen,
  tiny bubbles visible in the surface texture
- The whole design shifts and warps very slightly, as liquid does

MATCH THE PET
Keep the pet recognizable within these constraints:
- The overall head shape, muzzle length and ear type must match the
  attached photo
- Large markings become simple brown patches in roughly the same
  positions — a dark ear, a patch over one eye, a white blaze down the
  face
- Do not attempt fine fur detail. Simplify boldly. The likeness comes
  from silhouette and patch placement, not detail.

THE TECHNIQUE — build it according to the 방식 chosen above

· 프리푸어 (free pour) — A flowing, minimal design created purely by
  pouring. Soft rounded shapes, visible pour rings and layered ripples
  radiating outward. The dog reads as a simple silhouette or a bold
  two-tone face. Very few elements. Elegant and loose.

· 에칭 아트 (etching) — A detailed portrait scratched into the foam with
  a thin tool. Fine brown lines drawn through the white foam reveal the
  crema beneath, defining the eyes, nose, mouth and fur direction. The
  lines are slightly ragged and hand-drawn, with tiny foam ridges pushed
  up along their edges. Warm brown lines on white, never black ink.

· 3D 폼 아트 (3D foam) — A small sculpted dog built up in thick stiff
  foam, rising several centimetres above the surface. Rounded soft
  volumes, a fluffy cloud-like texture, tiny cocoa-powder dots for the
  eyes and nose. It sits in the cup looking up. Slightly wobbly and
  imperfect, clearly made of foam.

THE CUP AND SCENE
- A wide, shallow ceramic cup in plain matte cream or soft grey, on a
  matching saucer
- The cup fills most of the frame, shot from a high angle looking almost
  straight down, tilted just enough to see the rim and a little of the
  side
- A small teaspoon resting on the saucer, a linen napkin, a few scattered
  coffee beans on the wooden table
- Warm light wood table surface

LIGHT AND CAMERA
Soft natural window light from one side, gentle highlights on the foam
surface and a soft shadow from the cup. Shallow depth of field with the
foam art in sharp focus and the table edges falling soft. Warm, cozy cafe
atmosphere. Natural color, fine grain, no filters.

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

STYLE
A real photograph of a real cup of coffee, shot by a cafe photographer.
Warm and inviting. It must look like actual milk foam — never a printed
image, never a sticker, never a digital illustration on top of coffee.
