(설명하지 말고 바로 이미지를 만들어줘)

Redraw the attached photo as a simple hand-drawn colored pencil sketch on
white paper. Vertical 4:5 canvas.

THE LINEWORK
- Thin, clean black pen outlines with a consistent light weight
- The lines are confident and accurate — the proportions, pose and
  silhouette match the photo correctly. This is a competent drawing, not
  a childish one.
- Lines are slightly wobbly and imperfect in a natural hand-drawn way,
  but never sloppy or broken
- Minimal detail: only the essential contours. No cross-hatching, no
  rendering, no shadow lines.
- Simple dot eyes with a small white highlight, a small rounded nose, a
  simple curved smile with the tongue as a small pink shape

THE COLORING — this is the defining quality
- Colored in with loose diagonal PENCIL HATCHING: many parallel back-and-
  forth strokes running at a slant, clearly visible as individual strokes
- The strokes are quick and casual, with the white paper showing through
  between them — the color is never solid or fully opaque
- Coloring stays roughly inside the outlines, with only tiny overshoots
  here and there
- Pressure varies naturally: slightly darker where strokes overlap,
  lighter at the edges
- Use only ONE flat tone per area — no blending, no gradients, no shading
- For a fluffy coat, let the hatching strokes curve and swirl to follow
  the direction of the fur

MATCH THE PET
Use the pet's actual coat colors as the pencil colors. Recreate large
markings as separate colored areas in the same positions. Match the ear
type, the coat length and the pose from the photo. If there are multiple
pets, include all of them in the same arrangement as the photo.

THE BACKGROUND — leave it uncolored
This is important: redraw the background objects from the photo — the
furniture, bowls, appliances, doorways, whatever is there — but draw them
in OUTLINE ONLY, with no color at all. Just clean black pen lines on
bare white paper. Simplify them heavily into a few basic shapes.
The contrast between the colored pets and the colorless line-drawn
background is the whole point of the style.

THE PAPER
Plain white paper filling the frame, with a very faint natural texture.
A thin simple black rectangular border framing the whole drawing, with a
small white margin outside it.

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, signature, logos or watermarks anywhere.

STYLE
A simple, warm, minimal illustration — the kind of casual drawing someone
would make of their own pets. Clean, charming and unfussy. Never
photorealistic, never digital-looking, never heavily rendered.
