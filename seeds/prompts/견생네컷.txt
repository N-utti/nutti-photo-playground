Using the attached pet photo, create a Korean photo-booth style
"life four cut" (인생네컷) print featuring this exact pet.

LIKENESS (most important)
All four frames must show the SAME individual pet from the attached photo —
identical coat color, markings, ear shape, muzzle length and eye color.
Photorealistic, not illustrated. Clearly recognizable as this specific pet
in every frame.

LAYOUT — vertical 4:5 canvas
- Solid background in the color specified above, filling the whole canvas
- A 2x2 grid of four square photo frames centered, evenly spaced with a
  narrow uniform gap between them
- Generous margin at the top and bottom

TOP AREA
- The pet's name centered at the top, in pure white.
- Use an extra-bold rounded display font: very thick strokes, fully
  rounded soft terminals, slightly condensed, playful and friendly.
- If the name is written in Korean, render it in Korean using a heavy
  rounded Korean display typeface, spelled EXACTLY as given, character
  for character. Do not translate or alter it.
- No other text at the top.

BOTTOM AREA
- The text "nutti.co.kr" centered at the bottom, pure white, smaller than
  the name at the top
- Use the same extra-bold rounded bubbly font style — thick strokes,
  soft rounded terminals, chunky and cute
- Nothing else in the bottom area. No dots, no decorations, no icons.

THE FOUR FRAMES
Each frame is a head-and-chest studio portrait of the pet on a soft
cream-beige seamless backdrop, warm even lighting, shallow depth of field.
Same pet, same backdrop, four different moments:

Frame 1 (top left)    — head tilted playfully to one side, mouth open with
                        tongue slightly out, bright cheerful expression
Frame 2 (top right)   — nose pressed very close to the camera lens in a
                        playful wide-angle "boop" shot, nose slightly soft
                        focus, eyes visible behind it
Frame 3 (bottom left) — head tilted at a gentle angle to the opposite
                        side from Frame 1, looking straight into the lens
                        with a curious, inquisitive expression, mouth
                        closed, eyes soft and natural.
                        IMPORTANT: keep the ears in exactly the same
                        natural shape, length and position as in the
                        attached photo — do not perk, raise, or reshape
                        them. The head, muzzle and ear silhouette must
                        match the attached photo precisely.
Frame 4 (bottom right)— surrounded by floating translucent soap bubbles,
                        mouth open mid-play, eyes following one bubble

TEXT RENDERING
Only two pieces of text exist in the whole image: the pet's name at the
top and "nutti.co.kr" at the bottom. Do not add captions, dates,
watermarks, logos, or any other text anywhere.

STYLE
Clean photo-booth print aesthetic, vivid but soft colors, professional
pet studio photography quality.
