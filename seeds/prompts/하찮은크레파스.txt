Draw the pet in the attached photo as a crayon drawing made by a
3-year-old child. Vertical 4:5 canvas.

THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT PART — DRAW IT BADLY, ON PURPOSE
This must look like a real drawing by a very young child, NOT a
professional illustration imitating one. Absolutely no polish.
- Lines are wobbly, shaky and uneven, drawn slowly with a heavy hand
- Lines do not connect properly — gaps at the corners, overshooting ends
- Coloring is scribbled roughly and goes WAY outside the outlines,
  with white paper showing through in patches
- Waxy crayon texture: thick streaky strokes, uneven pressure, visible
  paper grain
- The body is a lopsided blob. Legs are four crooked sticks of different
  lengths. The tail is one squiggly line.
- The head is far too big for the body
- Eyes are two uneven dots at slightly different heights
- The nose is one big scribbled blob
- The mouth is a single crooked curved line
- The whole figure is slightly tilted and off-center on the page

NO cute cartoon styling. NO clean vector shapes. NO smooth gradients.
NO shading. NO perspective. NO professional line art of any kind.
If it looks skilled or designed, it is wrong.

KEEP THE PET RECOGNIZABLE (but crudely)
Even though it is drawn badly, a friend should still guess which dog it is:
- Use the pet's actual coat colors, scribbled in roughly
- If the pet has markings or patches, scribble them in the same rough
  positions (a dark patch over one eye, a white chest, etc.)
- Match the ear type in the crudest possible way: droopy ears become two
  long floppy blobs hanging down, upright ears become two lopsided
  triangles on top
- Match the coat length crudely: a fluffy dog gets messy scribbled fuzz
  around the outline, a short-haired dog gets a smooth-ish blob outline
- If the pet wears a collar, add it as one crooked colored line around
  the neck

PAPER AND BACKGROUND
- Drawn on slightly textured off-white sketchbook paper, filling the frame
- A few random stray crayon marks and smudges elsewhere on the page
- A wobbly green scribble line along the bottom for grass
- A lopsided yellow sun with uneven spiky rays in one top corner
- Optional: one or two crooked cloud squiggles

TEXT
No text, no letters, no numbers, no signature anywhere in the image.

FINAL CHECK
The result should be genuinely clumsy and endearing — the kind of drawing
a parent would proudly stick on the refrigerator. Charming because it is
badly drawn, not despite it.
