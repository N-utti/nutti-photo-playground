Using the attached pet photo, create a two-panel "if my dog were a human"
comparison portrait.

LAYOUT — vertical 4:5 canvas
- Solid background in the color specified above, filling the canvas
- Two equal-size panels stacked vertically, narrow uniform gap between them
- Even margin on all four sides. Nothing touches the canvas edge.

PANEL 1 (top) — THE DOG
A clean head-and-chest studio portrait of the pet from the attached photo.
Keep it photorealistic and identical to the attached photo: same coat
color, same markings and their placement, same ear type and shape, same
muzzle, same eye color, same expression. Soft cream-beige seamless
backdrop, warm even lighting.

PANEL 2 (bottom) — THE HUMAN VERSION
A photorealistic human portrait, matching the person type specified above.
Same framing, same backdrop, same lighting as Panel 1.

TRANSLATE the dog's features into human features — this is the whole point.
Do this deliberately, not randomly:
- Coat color  → hair color (a golden coat becomes warm blonde-brown hair,
                a black coat becomes glossy black hair, a cream coat
                becomes soft light hair)
- Coat texture→ hair texture (fluffy curly coat becomes wavy or permed
                hair, short sleek coat becomes straight neat hair)
- Ear shape   → hairstyle (long drooping ears become long hair framing
                the face, upright pointed ears become short cropped hair
                with a bit of lift)
- Face markings → subtle freckles, a beauty mark, or a lighter hair
                streak in the same position as the dog's markings
- Eye color and eye shape → carried over as closely as possible
- Expression and vibe → the same personality in the face. A goofy
                open-mouth dog becomes a bright wide grin; a calm serious
                dog becomes a composed gentle look.
- Collar color → the color of the person's simple knit top or scarf

The human must look like a real, natural, ordinary attractive person —
NOT a dog-human hybrid. No fur, no snout, no animal ears, no whiskers,
no makeup effects. Just a normal human whose look clearly echoes the dog.

TEXT
No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

STYLE
Warm natural studio portrait photography, soft light, shallow depth of
field, gentle film-like color grading. Both panels must look like they
were shot in the same session by the same photographer.
