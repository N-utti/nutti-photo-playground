IDENTITY LOCK

THE MIRROR SELFIE STRUCTURE — get this right
The entire image IS the mirror's reflection. We are looking at what the

- A small bright flash reflection blooms off the mirror where the camera

- A plain phone case with no branding, no text, no logos
