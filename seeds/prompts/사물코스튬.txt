THE COSTUME STRUCTURE — this is the whole joke
The pet is not wearing clothes. It is INSIDE a soft plush object costume.

THE OBJECT DESIGN
Build the costume as a simplified, cute, plush interpretation of the
object written above:
- Made of soft fleece or felt with visible fabric texture and seam lines
- Rounded, chubby proportions — nothing sharp or angular
- Simple flat color blocking, with the object's key identifying features

  green husk, a fire extinguisher's red body and nozzle)
- The top of the object usually forms a hood or cap sitting over the head,
  above the face opening
- Slightly imperfect and handmade: soft lumps, a crooked seam, uneven
